HOW TO PLAY

Controls

W A S D / ←↑↓→ move
SPACE or mouse click: scan pulse (15 energy)
P / Esc pause

Scan pulse

Extracts amber samples, stuns drones for 2.5s and activates beacons.

Survive

Oxygen drains constantly: grab O₂ canisters. Medkits repair hull. Crystals recharge energy.

Hazards

Toxic vents (pulse, then burst), drones, drifting asteroids and gravity wells.

Objectives

Finish every objective, then fly into the warp gate. Stars: 1 for finishing, +1 for hull ≥ 50%, +1 for beating par time.

Fact vs Fiction

Fact cards tagged REAL SCIENCE are true. Cards tagged GAME FICTION explain what the game invents.
